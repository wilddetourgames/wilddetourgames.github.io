Liquid Logic Privacy Policy

Wild Detour Games · Effective 25 October 2026

In short
  • Liquid Logic does not collect, sell or share any personal information.
  • There is no account or sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Reminder notifications are optional and are created on your device.

Who we are

Liquid Logic is a color sorting puzzle game published on Google Play by Wild Detour Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the game handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or device information. The game does not send your data to us or to anyone else, and it contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device only: levels completed, stars, moves, coins, themes you own or use, daily challenge and streak progress, achievements, and your settings (sound, animation, color assist and reminders). This information never leaves your device through the game. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the game's saved progress in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is created and stored by Google under your Google account, and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. We cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

Notifications

The game can show an optional daily puzzle reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no information is sent anywhere to create them. Android asks for your permission first, and you can turn reminders off at any time in the game's Settings or in your device's settings.

Third-party services

The game is distributed through Google Play, which handles downloads, updates and any reviews you choose to leave, under Google's Privacy Policy. The game is built with the Unity engine; Unity's analytics, crash-reporting, advertising and other online services are not enabled in Liquid Logic.

Children

Liquid Logic is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Because the game does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that handle information differently, for example advertising, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.

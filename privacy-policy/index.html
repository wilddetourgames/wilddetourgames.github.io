Liquid Logic Privacy Policy

Wild Detour Games · Effective 29 October 2026

In short
  • We (Wild Detour Games) do not collect, store or sell any personal information.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • There is no account or sign-in, and your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Reminder notifications are optional and are created on your device.

Who we are

Liquid Logic is a color sorting puzzle game published on Google Play by Wild Detour Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the game handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The game has no account, does not send your data to us, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. The advertising section below explains what Google collects to show ads.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device only: levels completed, stars, moves, coins, themes you own or use, daily challenge and streak progress, achievements, and your settings (sound, animation, color assist and reminders). This information never leaves your device through the game. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the game's saved progress in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is created and stored by Google under your Google account, and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. We cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

Notifications

The game can show an optional daily puzzle reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no information is sent anywhere to create them. Android asks for your permission first, and you can turn reminders off at any time in your device's notification settings (or by long-pressing a reminder).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, it shows ads from Google AdMob: a short full-screen ad between some levels (never during a puzzle, and never in the first levels), a small banner on menu screens and, after the first levels, below the puzzle controls, and an optional video ad you can choose to watch for a free Extra Tube. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google: your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate its general location), interactions with ads such as views and taps, diagnostic information such as app launch time and performance, and device identifiers including the Android advertising ID and app set ID. This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings under "Privacy choices". On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads).

Third-party services

The game is distributed through Google Play, which handles downloads, updates and any reviews you choose to leave, under Google's Privacy Policy. Ads are provided by Google AdMob as described above. The game is built with the Unity engine; Unity's analytics, crash-reporting, advertising and other online services are not enabled in Liquid Logic.

Children

Liquid Logic is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security and deletion

Because we do not collect or hold personal information, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed or deleted. Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored on your device. For data Google collects through ads, use your Google account and device settings described above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game handles information differently, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
